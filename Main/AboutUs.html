ABOUT US

At KapeBara, we believe life is better when you slow down and relax. Inspired by the world's most chill creature — the Capybara — our coffee shop is designed to be a sanctuary from the daily grind. Whether you're looking for a bold morning brew, a creamy ice-cold frappe, or a tasty snack to fuel your day, we have the perfect blend for every mood.

Since Day One

OUR
STORY

KapeBara began with a mission to bring the chill culture to the local coffee scene. Why the capybara? Because in a world full of hustle, we choose to be the calm. We started with a passion for the perfect brew and a dream of a space where students could study, friends could bond, and anyone could unwind. Today, we take pride in our hand-crafted coffees, signature frappes, and curated snacks — all served in an environment that feels like home. No rush. No stress. Just Kape Bara.

Find Us

123 Capybara Street, Ormoc City

Our coffee shop is conveniently located in the heart of the city, making it the perfect place for students, professionals, and families to relax and unwind. Surrounded by a peaceful neighborhood and easy-to-access roads, our café offers a warm and cozy atmosphere where everyone can enjoy freshly brewed coffee and meaningful moments together.
