Payment Options

Welcome to KapeBara

Type of Payment Options

Credit Card — We accept all major Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards. Just enter your card details at checkout and you're good to go. All transactions are encrypted and secured so you don't have to worry about your information going anywhere it shouldn't. It's quick, reliable, and works every single time as long as your card is active and has enough available credit.

GCash — Probably the most popular choice among our customers and honestly we get why. GCash is super fast, super convenient, and almost everyone has it on their phone already. At checkout just choose GCash, enter your registered mobile number, approve the payment request on your GCash app, and boom your order is confirmed. No cash, no hassle, no stress.

PayMaya — Another solid e-wallet option for those who prefer it. PayMaya works pretty much the same way as GCash. Select it at checkout, confirm the payment on your PayMaya app, and your order gets processed right away. It's smooth, secure, and takes literally seconds to complete.

BPI Online — For those who prefer to keep things through their bank, we've got you. You can pay directly using your BPI online banking account without needing to go to an ATM or branch. Just select BPI Online at checkout, log in to your BPI account, and authorize the payment. It's a great option if you like having everything tracked through your bank statement.
