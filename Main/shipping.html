How our delivery work?

We currently deliver your KapeBara orders straight to your door through our trusted local delivery partners. Once you place your order, our team carefully packs your items fresh and hands them off to the rider. Delivery usually takes around 30 to 60 minutes depending on your location and how busy we are we know you don't want to wait too long for your coffee! You'll get a notification once your order is on its way, so just sit back, relax, and let the caffeine come to you. For now, we deliver within Quezon City only, but we're working on expanding soon!
