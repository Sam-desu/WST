Type of Order Status

Pending — We got your order and we're getting things ready! Our team is brewing and packing your items. Just hang tight, it won't be long.

Completed — Your order has been delivered and everything went smoothly. We hope you're enjoying every sip! If something wasn't right, feel free to reach out to us.

Canceled — Something came up and your order wasn't able to push through. This could be because an item ran out of stock, there was an issue with your payment, or you requested a cancellation yourself. Don't worry if you were charged, a refund will be processed shortly.
